# Google Play listing — English (United States)

## App name

HRV AI

## Short description

Adaptive paced breathing guided by R-R intervals from a Polar chest strap.

## Full description

HRV AI is a calm paced-breathing companion that uses beat-to-beat timing from a compatible Polar chest strap to estimate how strongly and regularly your heart-rate wave follows the breathing cue.

Choose a fixed breathing pace, compare several rates through guided calibration, or let the conservative adaptive mode explore small changes near your established pace. The app waits for qualified signal windows and holds steady when contact, timing, or data quality is insufficient.

Features:

• Direct Polar H9 Bluetooth connection
• Live heart rate and R-R interval diagnostics
• Fixed and adaptive paced-breathing sessions
• 50/50 and 40/60 inhale-to-exhale timing
• Optional sound and haptic cues
• Guided multi-rate calibration
• Local session history and signal-quality summaries
• Raw and corrected R-R export
• Per-session and complete data deletion
• No account, ads, analytics, or cloud health-data storage

HRV AI does not measure respiration directly. It knows the cue it displays and estimates likely coupling from the resulting heart-rate pattern. Poor-quality data is marked as inconclusive rather than treated as a wellness score.

Requires a compatible Polar chest strap capable of providing R-R intervals. The Polar H9 is the primary supported sensor.

HRV AI is experimental wellness and biofeedback software. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, prevent, monitor, or treat any medical condition. Stop paced breathing and return to comfortable natural breathing if you feel dizzy, tingly, air hungry, uncomfortable, or notice unusual symptoms. Seek appropriate medical care for urgent or persistent symptoms.
