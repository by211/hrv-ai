# HRV AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 14, 2026

HRV AI is a local-first wellness and biofeedback application published by byAI. This policy explains how the Android application handles information.

## Information processed by the app

When you connect a compatible Polar chest strap, HRV AI processes heart rate, beat-to-beat R-R intervals, chest-contact status, sensor device ID, signal quality, battery level, breathing-session settings, session timestamps, optional comfort ratings, and optional symptom flags.

## Local storage and collection

HRV AI stores session measurements and settings in the application's private storage on your device. The developer does not receive, collect, or transmit this information to a server. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics, or cloud synchronization.

The app records limited connection diagnostics locally, such as notification counts, the last connection event, and errors. Debug builds may also write connection events to Android Logcat. These diagnostics are not transmitted by the app.

## Bluetooth access

HRV AI requests Android Nearby Devices permissions to discover and connect to a compatible Bluetooth Low Energy heart-rate sensor. The app does not request location permission and does not use Bluetooth scanning to determine your location.

## User-directed export

You may explicitly export a session as a file. The export contains session metadata and raw and corrected R-R measurements. After you choose an export destination or share the file, its handling is controlled by you and by the destination application or service you select.

## Retention and deletion

Information remains on your device until you delete an individual session, use the in-app delete-all-data control, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Android system backups are disabled for HRV AI.

## Third-party services

HRV AI uses Polar's Android Bluetooth SDK to communicate directly with the sensor. HRV AI does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, account, or health-data hosting services.

## Children's privacy

HRV AI is not directed to children under 13. It is intended for adults using a compatible heart-rate sensor for general wellness and biofeedback.

## Health and medical disclaimer

HRV AI is experimental wellness and biofeedback software, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, prevent, monitor, or treat disease and is not a substitute for professional medical care.

## Changes
